SCOWL (Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists), version 2020.12.07
by Kevin Atkinson (kevina@gnu.org)
http://wordlist.aspell.net/

common_english_words.txt is final/english-words sizes 10, 20, 35, 40, and
50 combined (SCOWL size 50, "medium"), filtered to words of 3 to 6 letters
made only of a-z. No words were added by hand.
